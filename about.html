About Us

Discover the story behind Bliss Makeover Studio and Academy and meet our talented team

Our Story

Founded in July 2026, Bliss Makeover Studio and Academy began as a dream to create a space where beauty meets artistry, and every client feels like the most beautiful version of themselves.

What started as a small home-based business has grown into Bulandshahr's premier beauty destination. Our founder, Priya Sharma, envisioned a studio that would not just provide makeup services, but create transformative experiences that boost confidence and celebrate individual beauty.

Over the years, we've had the privilege of being part of countless special moments - from intimate gatherings to grand weddings, from first dates to milestone celebrations. Each client's story becomes part of our story, and we're honored to play a role in making their special moments even more memorable.

Our Mission

To enhance natural beauty and boost confidence through professional makeup artistry and personalized beauty services.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative beauty studio, setting new standards in the industry.

Our Values

Quality, creativity, professionalism, and a commitment to making every client feel special and beautiful.
