Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

How far in advance should I book?

For bridal services, we recommend booking 2-3 months in advance, especially during peak wedding season. For other services, 1-2 weeks advance booking is usually sufficient.

Do you provide makeup trials?

Yes! We highly recommend trials for bridal makeup. This allows us to understand your preferences and ensure you're completely satisfied with your look before the big day.

What products do you use?

We use only premium, professional-grade makeup brands including MAC, Bobbi Brown, NARS, and other high-quality products that are safe for all skin types.

Do you travel for appointments?

Yes, we provide home service for an additional travel fee. This is especially popular for bridal packages and group bookings.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations. Cancellations made within 48 hours may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! We offer special rates for group bookings of 3 or more people. Contact us for custom pricing for your group.
