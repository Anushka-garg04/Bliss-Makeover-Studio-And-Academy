Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and policies

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling us at +91 94100 16655, using our online booking form, or visiting our studio. We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance for regular services and 2-3 months for bridal packages.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations. Cancellations made within 48 hours may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee. For bridal packages, we require 1 week notice for cancellations.

Do you provide home service?

Yes, we provide home service for an additional travel fee. This is especially popular for bridal packages and group bookings. Please contact us for pricing and availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, UPI payments, and digital wallets. For bridal packages, we require a 50% advance payment at the time of booking.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes, we offer special rates for group bookings of 3 or more people. Contact us for custom pricing and packages for your group.

What products do you use?

We use only premium, professional-grade makeup brands including MAC, Bobbi Brown, NARS, and other high-quality products that are safe for all skin types and provide long-lasting results.
